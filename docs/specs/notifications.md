# Notifications

Sources: toast surface [`notifications/index.tsx`](../../packages/widgets/src/notifications/index.tsx), center [`notification-center/index.tsx`](../../packages/widgets/src/notification-center/index.tsx), list/history [`notification-center/list.tsx`](../../packages/widgets/src/notification-center/list.tsx), shared card [`common/notification.tsx`](../../packages/widgets/src/common/notification.tsx).

## Toasts and center are separate surfaces

Toasts use the notification daemon through `useNotifd`, corner anchoring and passive `Keymode.NONE`; they do not take keyboard focus. They are visible only with an available daemon and toast count, while DND is off and the session is unlocked. At most three toasts are shown: criticals are pinned above ordinary notifications, newest first within each urgency band. Same-ID content updates keep their position, widget and remaining lifetime; an urgency change moves the item into its new priority band. Critical notifications do not auto-dismiss and ordinary chatter cannot evict them; when criticals alone exceed the cap, the newest three remain as toasts and the others remain available in the center. Noncritical expiry and optional progress use one monotonic countdown: hovering freezes both, and leaving resumes the remaining interval. Invoking an action or dismissing a toast removes it from the toast stack, not from history.

The toast layer is anchored to the upper-right corner of the focused monitor, regardless of bar position, with a 4px outer margin. Keep the corner anchor: adding both horizontal anchors would create an invisible full-width input surface. The toast-list spacing is an intentional 4px exception to the general 8px list spacing: each independent card owns its 8px inset, and the narrow inter-card gap stays transparent. No extra transparent gutter is added around the stack.

Toast height is bounded by the focused monitor's logical height, divided among the visible cards. Each card scrolls its own content inside its 8px inset; there is no stack scroller to clip the card edges or shadows. Visibility also yields while the center is open so the toast layer cannot cover its controls.

The notification center is independently routed by `ShellState.notificationsOpen` and opened from the bar/Quick Settings. It uses an opaque native window without an enclosing card, a 12px content inset and an on-demand keyboard mode. Its compact header contains the title, the native **Não perturbe** switch, Settings and a close button at the trailing edge. The header is measured after rooting, before the initial map, and remeasured on map and monitor changes. Live GDK monitor geometry supplies logical dimensions even when the center stays mapped during a mode change; Astal monitor synchronization supplies current reservations. Its scroll budget shares Quick Settings' pure budget calculation and subtracts the real header, surface insets, logical top/bottom reservations and 4px layer margins. Verify 900px→800px resizing without hiding/reopening the center; a remap alone does not establish live geometry updates. Settings opens notification preferences; the saved schedule is under **Não perturbe** in Settings. A single action row switches between active/history views and offers **Dispensar todas** or **Limpar histórico** with destructive styling. Closing or losing center focus closes it, except while its modal confirmation child is active. History clear requires explicit confirmation and does not change DND.

Scheduled DND stores its enabled state and hours in GSettings (default off, 22:00–07:00). The shell owns the timer, so closing Settings does not stop it. Manual changes take precedence until the next schedule boundary; disabling the schedule restores the previous mode only if the schedule still controls it.

## Shared card and history

Every shared card variant has an 8px inner inset and 8px content gaps, with 12px before its separated action section. The top row is a 16px app icon, app name and live relative time in `caption dimmed`, then a flat close button. Toast close buttons appear on hover/focus; center controls are always visible and keyboard-reachable. Toasts remain passive; the center is the keyboard route for reading, invoking and dismissing notifications.

Summaries use `heading`, at most two lines and an end ellipsis. Bodies use `body`, capped at three lines with an ellipsis for toasts and complete in the center. Both use word/character wrapping so long URLs cannot force the card wider. Valid Pango body markup is retained; invalid markup is displayed literally. Criticals have a leading warning symbol and an `error`-colored leading strip, not just a text tint. Low-urgency content keeps ordinary summary/body contrast.

Action buttons wrap, exclude the `default` action and empty labels, and do not reserve space for hidden actions. A sole visible action uses `suggested-action`; multiple actions have equal weight. Only a real `default` action makes the message area a native button with a pointer cursor; in the center it can also be invoked by keyboard. Invoking a center action dismisses its active notification while retaining history. Countdown progress is specific to timed toasts, not persistent center cards.

Active groups always have the same app heading and count, including when collapsed; empty active/history views retain their section heading and action row. History uses a single `Gtk.ListBoxRow` per entry, escapes all markup, retains the time of day for old entries, and expands the existing body label rather than duplicating its contents. **Mostrar mais** reveals entries after each initial group of twenty.


## System alerts and sounds

[`SystemAlerts`](../../packages/services/src/notifications/systemAlerts.ts) owns hardware observations; the GI-free [`alerts.ts`](../../packages/services/src/notifications/alerts.ts) owns decisions and notification text. Settings → **Notificações** groups visual controls under **Alertas do sistema** and playback controls under **Sons**. Each visual alert family is enabled by default, independently of its sound switch.

| Family | Defaults and rearming |
|---|---|
| Computer battery | Low at 20%, critical at 10%, only while discharging. The first valid reading is a silent baseline, even below a threshold. Each level rearms only after recovering 5 percentage points, with a 60-second per-level cooldown. Critical escalates immediately over low and uses the same notification replacement key. |
| Charger | One notification per observed plug/unplug transition; no startup announcement. Line-power devices are rediscovered after device changes rather than captured once at boot. |
| Local temperature | Warning at 80 °C, critical at 90 °C, rearming 5 °C below each threshold and a five-minute per-level cooldown. A sensor that stays hot never repeats just because the cooldown expires. Critical escalation is not delayed by a recent warning. Only local `SystemUsage` is used, converting its fraction back to actual Celsius; unavailable readings do not clear the latch. |
| Bluetooth connection | Device identity includes its adapter. Connect/disconnect must stay stable for at least one second; a quick reconnect cancels the pending disconnect. Existing connections are silent on startup. |
| Bluetooth battery | Low at 20%, per connected device; rearm at threshold +5 points with a 60-second cooldown. Reconnection does not reset an exhausted low-battery latch. Removed devices release cached Battery1 proxies; a bounded reconnect memory retains decision state for 30 minutes. |

Threshold ranges are battery low 10–50%, critical 1–20%; temperature warning 50–110 °C, critical 60–120 °C; Bluetooth battery 5–50%. Preference rows keep critical battery below low, and critical temperature above warning; decisions also normalize externally written conflicting pairs.

Alerts are real `org.freedesktop.Notifications.Notify` calls on the session bus. Returned IDs are retained per alert key and same-key requests are serialized, so a critical escalation updates the preceding alert instead of creating a duplicate. Critical battery/temperature use critical urgency; other alerts use normal urgency and symbolic icons. Device names appear only in bounded, plain-text bodies, never summaries. MAC addresses are removed, including from aliases. While locked with content hidden, newly generated Bluetooth bodies use generic device text. Notification-daemon ownership changes invalidate cached replacement IDs.

Sounds consume these same decisions, not separate threshold watchers or every UPower device event. Charger, Bluetooth, battery, local-temperature, capture, lock/unlock and notification arrivals have independent playback controls; volume-step feedback is opt-in and follows the current default speaker. All sounds, including critical sounds, respect the master switch and DND. Same-ID notification updates, ignored apps and restored notification backlog are silent. Shade system alerts use their specific sound, never an additional generic notification sound. Device/capture bursts are coalesced; a missing player or sound theme is silent. Playback accepts only the allowlisted freedesktop event IDs and invokes `canberra-gtk-play` through an argv array.

The GTK3 sound helper must not inherit the shell's GTK4 layer-shell preload. Playback removes only `libgtk4-layer-shell.so` entries from the child's `LD_PRELOAD` through `Gio.SubprocessLauncher`, preserving unrelated preloads and all other environment variables. It never changes the running shell's environment.

## Manual verification

On a suitable session, submit normal, low and critical notifications with long summaries, URLs, valid/invalid markup and several long action labels. Check light/dark contrast, 8px card insets, action wrapping, close-button hover/focus, and unclipped toast edges on a short or scaled monitor. Observe criticals above ordinary chatter, newest-first ordering within each priority band, and same-ID content updates preserving position, hover pause and progress. Expand the center on 800px and 900px logical-height monitors and verify its window stays inside the layer margins and actual top/bottom reservations. With progress enabled, hover beyond the original timeout and confirm that the remaining bar and expiry resume together. Leave the center open long enough to see its relative times advance. Use Tab/Enter/Space to invoke actual default/actions and dismiss center cards; non-actionable text must not show a pointer. Toggle DND and lock state; verify toast visibility changes without asserting daemon-side retention. Check active/history empty states, collapsed counts, literal history markup, expansion without duplicate text, old timestamps, **Mostrar mais**, and clear/cancel confirmation. These are proposed checks, not new VM results.
